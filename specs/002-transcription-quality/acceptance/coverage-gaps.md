# Benchmark coverage gaps

## Authentic within-speaker Slovak/English switching

Open. No suitable public dataset with verified evaluation/redistribution rights has been established in this review. Common Voice monolingual clips and VoxPopuli Slovak-accented English do not establish within-speaker switching. Legacy concatenations are synthetic stress tests. They must never be relabeled or counted toward this gap.

This gap does not block T013/T014 public-corpus baseline closure. It does prevent claims about authentic switching quality or satisfaction of the inherited mixed-language accuracy target. Future coverage must identify actual within-speaker switching, source rights, reference text and switch boundaries in both directions.

## Other limitations

Read speech and parliamentary speech do not represent personal microphone dictation. Technology keyword matches are a topical subset, not necessarily dense software identifiers. Entity/numeric selection is based on recorded transcript evidence, not inferred speaker identities. The separate long-form VoxPopuli corpus now covers continuous natural source intervals from 60.260 to 176.139 seconds, including two above 170 seconds. It closes the chunk-planning duration gap but does not establish personal-dictation or authentic code-switching quality. Human meaning review remains separate.

## Public source search

The [2019 Slovak-English bilingual ASR paper](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-2473/paper28.pdf)
describes about 122 minutes assembled from several kinds of speech. The paper alone does not
provide a verified licensed downloadable evaluation package. The
[Slovak National Corpus spoken interface](https://korpus.juls.savba.sk/shk_en.html) describes
registered query access, not a rights-cleared bulk audio download for this benchmark. Neither
was scraped or treated as licensed acquisition. No suitable open same-speaker Slovak/English
package was established in this search; this is a documented search result, not a claim that
none exists.
